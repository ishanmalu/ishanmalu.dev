  • SeedscapeWorlds · 2026live A Minecraft Java seed explorer that puts everything on one map: biomes, structures, strongholds and slime chunks, with shaded relief and a 3D terrain view. It all runs in your browser.
  • DaisyMac · 2026macOS Hold Shift, drag a file and drop it on a petal. Daisy shows every format that file can become, right where your cursor is, and converts it on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • CadenceApps · 2026coming soon Habits, sleep, training and meals in one steady rhythm. It imports Oura data and writes you a weekly training plan.
  • PerchMac · 2025macOS A small menu bar utility that stays out of the way until you need it. Built without Xcode, using only SwiftPM.
  • ClipmineTools · 2026runs on my Mac Paste a TikTok, Reels or YouTube link and get a clean transcript. Or give it a topic: it pulls the videos that actually performed, mines their opening hooks and writes new ones.
  • PrompterTools · 2026experiment A teleprompter that records. The script floats over the camera but never ends up in the video. One HTML file, no build step.
